# ConnectAI Platform

Create a modern, premium front-facing landing page for an AI Website Connector platform. The website itself is the product — do not design an AI chatbot interface.

Visual Style

Futuristic but clean SaaS aesthetic

Dark background with subtle gradients

Black/deep navy base with electric blue, purple, and cyan accents

Glassmorphism cards with soft borders and glow effects

Smooth animations and micro-interactions

Professional enough for a real technology startup

Responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile

Hero Section

Large headline:

“Connect Your Website to AI.”

Subheadline:

“Power your website with intelligent AI integrations — without rebuilding your entire stack.”

Add two CTA buttons:

Connect Your Website

Explore Integrations

Include a large futuristic visual showing a website in the center connected through glowing lines to different services, APIs, databases, and AI systems.

Navigation

Top navigation:

Logo: ConnectAI

Product

Integrations

How It Works

Pricing

Documentation

Get Started button

Features Section

Create 3–4 visually impressive cards:

Easy Integration — Connect existing websites with minimal setup.

Smart Connections — Link websites to APIs, databases, and AI services.

Real-Time Data — Keep connected systems synchronized.

Secure by Design — Modern authentication and secure data handling.

How It Works

Show a simple 3-step process:

01 — Connect
Enter your website or choose an integration.

02 — Configure
Choose what services and data your website should connect to.

03 — Launch
Activate the connection and start using your integrated website.

Integration Showcase

Display recognizable integration-style cards for:

Websites

APIs

Databases

Analytics

CRM

AI services

Automation tools

Use glowing connection lines between them to visually communicate that the platform connects everything together.

Bottom CTA

Large closing section:

“Your website. Connected to everything.”

Subtext:
“Build smarter digital experiences without the complexity.”

Button:
Start Connecting →

Important

The design should feel like the landing page of a serious AI infrastructure/SaaS company, not an AI chat website. Avoid chat bubbles, conversation screens, robot faces, or chatbot layouts. Focus on website connectivity, integrations, automation, APIs, and a polished futuristic SaaS brand.
